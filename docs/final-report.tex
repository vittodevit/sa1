\subsection{Collaboration and communication}
\label{sec:collaboration}

The project involved several groups working on different sections of the same website. Communication between members and topic leaders was therefore important for keeping the work organized and ensuring that the different contributions could eventually be integrated.

The level of communication varied between groups and individuals. While some members regularly shared feedback, asked questions, and communicated when they encountered difficulties, others were occasionally unresponsive to messages in the group chats. This sometimes resulted in requested changes and modifications to HTML pages being overlooked or left unimplemented, despite reminders being sent. Consequently, the team leaders sometimes had to intervene and apply the necessary corrections themselves to ensure that the pages met the project's requirements.

Creating group chats and maintaining direct communication helped address some of these difficulties. The topic leaders also played an important role in coordinating their members, clarifying assignments, collecting contributions, following up on requested modifications, and ensuring that the content of each section was complete.


\subsection{Website consistency and corrections}
\label{sec:corrections}

One of the main challenges during development was maintaining a consistent
structure and visual appearance across all the article pages.

The CSS leaders provided a template and shared styling structure intended to
be followed by everyone when creating their pages. However, some members did
not follow the provided template consistently. As a result, different pages
were developed using different layouts and structures, even though they were
part of the same website.

This created additional work during integration. Several pages had to be
reviewed and corrected to make their structure, appearance, and functionality
consistent with the rest of the website. Other issues, including incorrect
HTML structure, image paths, CSS links, and navigation, also required attention.

These corrections required cooperation between the team leaders, the CSS
leaders, and the members responsible for the affected pages. The experience
highlighted the importance of following shared guidelines when multiple people
are developing different parts of the same website.

\subsection{Overall assessment}
\label{sec:assessment}

Despite the difficulties encountered during development, the team produced a
website bringing together the contributions of the different topic groups.
The project required not only research and HTML development, but also
coordination, shared design decisions, version control, technical corrections,
and continuous integration.

The main challenges concerned communication, adherence to the shared page
template, and the amount of correction required before the pages could be
combined consistently. At the same time, several members demonstrated
initiative and reliability by providing feedback, supporting their groups,
taking on additional responsibilities, and responding promptly when changes
were requested, while others were less focused, had less participation when asked to meet or
when changes were supposed to be made.

The work of both team leaders was particularly important to the completion of
the project: Angelica focused on deadlines, communication, feedback, and
coordination, while Vittorio took responsibility for Git, technical corrections,
and the integration and functionality of the website. The topic leaders also
contributed by organizing their groups, guiding the work, and delivering their
respective sections.

Overall, the project provided practical experience in teamwork, task
distribution, web development, version control, and the challenges of
integrating contributions produced independently by a large group.
