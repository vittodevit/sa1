Single Sign-On and Social Login

Many websites let you sign in using an account you already have, such as Google, Apple, or Facebook. This is convenient because you don’t need to create another password. But if that account is compromised, it could give someone access to other services you use.

What is single sign-on?

Single sign-on means logging in once through a trusted service to access several other services. Social login is a familiar example, you use a button such as "Sign in with Google" to sign in to another website.

Example: SWITCH edu-ID at USI

At USI, a SWITCH edu-ID account gives access to services including iCorsi and the library. These services use the same university identity, so you don’t need a separate account for each one.
