Account Recovery and Password Resets

Everyone forgets a password sometimes, so websites offer a "Forgot password?" option. If an attacker can misuse it they may access your account without knowing your password. That makes account recovery a possible back door into your account.

How a password reset works

Here's what usually happens when you reset a password:

  1. Step 1

    You click "Forgot password?"

  2. Step 2

    You enter your email address or phone number.

  3. Step 3

    The website sends you a reset link or code.

  4. Step 4

    You use it to choose a new password and regain access.

Whoever controls the email account or phone number used for recovery may be able to reset your password.
