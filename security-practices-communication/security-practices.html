Good Security Practices

Most cyberattacks do not require a genius hacker. They succeed because people reuse passwords, postpone software updates, or keep no backups. Adopting a few simple daily habits stops most of them.

Why everyday habits matter

According to the 2025 Verizon Data Breach Investigations Report (DBIR), a human action was involved in about 60% of breaches. The most common entry points were stolen passwords (22%), followed by unpatched software vulnerabilities (20%).
