Email security

Usual email is similar to a postcard. It is usually encrypted during transfer, but providers may still be able to read the content.

Postcard vs sealed letter

Normal email: like a postcard

End-to-end encrypted email:like a sealed letter.

alt="Email security illustration">

Secure email methods help protect messages from unauthorized access.

Email security methods

Methods What it does
TLS Encrypts between servers, but providers can still read.
PGP Only the recipient can decrypt the message.
S/MIME Encryption and signatures using certificates.
SPF / DKIM / DMARC Helps verify the sender's domain.

Since February 2024, Gmail and Yahoo have intoduced stricter email authentication requirements for bulk senders.

Example

Sending a scan of your ID throigh normal email may leave readable copies on multiple services. A secure upload portal is a safer option.

Quick tips

  • Check the real sender address.
  • Do not email passwords or identity documents.
  • Use encrypted email for sensitive information.
