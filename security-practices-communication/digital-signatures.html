Digital signatures and certificates

A digital signature helps to prove who sent a document and that its content was not modified since. A certificate connects a pubic key to an identity.

Digital signatures help verify the sender and protect the integrity of the document.

How digital signatures work

1. Sign with private key.

2. Send the document and signature.

3. Verify with public key.

Certificates

  • Certificate:An electronic ID card linking a public key to a name.
  • Certificate Authority: A trusted organization that issues certificates.
  • Chain of trust: Firstly website, then certificateAuthority and finally browser.

Where they are used

Used in What it proves
HTTPS websites The website controls the doamin
Software updates Real developer, file not modifies
Signed PDFs Who signed and that nothing changed

Example

Before installing a software update, the computer can check its digital signature. If the file was changed the verification will fail.

Useful tips

  • Be careful with inknown developer warnings.
  • Check certificate information in your browser.
  • Install software from official sources.
