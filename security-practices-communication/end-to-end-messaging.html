End-to-end encrypted messaging

The end-to-end encryption system (E2EE) allows only the sender and recipient to read messages, and not the messaging company itself.

Encryption in transit vs E2EE

Encryption in transit End-to-end encryption
Link betwen you and the server. Link between sender and receiver.
Provider can access content Provider cannot read the content of the message.
Usual email, most websites Signal, WhatsApp, iMessage

How E2EE works

Sender locks the message and then server transfers it to the receiver. After that only the receiver can unlock it.

What E2EE does not protect

  • Metadata, such as who communicates and when.
  • Unencrypted cloud backups.
  • Unlocked or infected devices.
  • Screenshots taken by the receiver.

Real-world example: WhatsApp

In April 2016, WhatsApp introduced end-to-end encryption for more than one billion users, with the help of the Signal Protocol.

Useful tips

  • Use E2EE apps for private conversations.
  • Enable encrypted backups.
  • Always lock your phone.
