Software updates

An update is often a security fix. Waiting gives attackers time. Software has bugs, and some let attackers in (vulnerabilities). A fix is called a patch. Once a patch is public, attackers know where the hole is.

Promptly applying patches closes vulnerabilities before attackers can exploit them.

What to do

  • Turn on automatic updates for the system, browser, and apps.
  • Restart your device when asked.
  • Replace devices that no longer receive security updates.
  • Only update from official sources, never from a website pop-up.

Real example: WannaCry

Date What happened
14 March 2017 Microsoft releases the fix (MS17-010).
12 May 2017 WannaCry hits 200,000+ unpatched computers in 150 countries.
Same week UK hospitals turn away patients due to unpatched systems.

The full story of WannaCry is told in Famous Incidents.

Quick tips

  • Turn on automatic updates.
  • Restart when your device asks.
  • Don't use devices without security updates.

Source: UpGuard (WannaCry); Verizon DBIR 2025
