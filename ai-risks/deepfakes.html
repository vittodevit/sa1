Deepfakes and AI Impersonation

A familiar voice or face can make a scam convincing. AI tools can copy these features, so a recording alone is not enough to check someone's identity.

What is a deepfake?

A deepfake is media made or changed using AI to make a person appear to say or do something that did not really happen.

A common example is a fake voice that sounds like a friend, family member or colleague.

Why is it a security risk?

Someone may use a fake video or voice to pretend to be another person and ask for money or private information.

For example, a person might receive an urgent call that sounds like a relative asking for money. The voice alone does not prove who is calling.

How can we stay safe?

It is important to check unusual messages before acting on them.

  • Call the person back using a number you already know.
  • Be careful with urgent requests for money or passwords.
  • Check information with another trusted source.
