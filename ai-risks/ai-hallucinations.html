AI Hallucinations and Overreliance in Cybersecurity

AI can help explain security topics, but a clear answer is not always a correct one. Problems arise when people follow it without checking.

What is an AI hallucination?

A hallucination happens when AI generates incorrect information and presents it as if it were true.

For example, a chatbot might invent a command or describe a security update that does not exist.

Why is overreliance dangerous?

Overreliance means trusting a tool too much without checking its work. In cybersecurity, wrong information can lead to poor decisions.

Someone might copy an AI-generated command while trying to protect a computer. If the advice is wrong, the change could make the computer less secure.

How can we reduce mistakes?

  • Check important facts using official documentation.
  • Read and understand a suggested command before running it.
  • Do not use AI as the only source for security decisions.
