Major Data Breaches

Data breaches happen when unauthorized people gain access to private or confidential information. They can affect millions of individuals, companies and organizations. Some of the most significant examples are Yahoo, Equifax and Marriott.

1. Yahoo Data Breaches

Background

Yahoo experienced two major data breaches, in 2013 and 2014. These incidents affected billions of user accounts and became some of the largest data breaches in history.

How did they happen?

In the 2013 incident, attackers gained access to Yahoo's user account database. The exact method of the attack has never been fully established. In 2014, attackers used stolen information and forged authentication cookies to access user accounts without needing their passwords.

Impact

The 2013 breach affected approximately 3 billion accounts, while the 2014 breach affected around 500 million accounts. Exposed information included names, email addresses, telephone numbers and passwords.

Why is it important?

The Yahoo incidents showed how a single company's security failures can affect billions of people. They also demonstrated the importance of protecting user information and informing customers when a breach occurs.

2. Equifax Data Breach (2017)

Background

Equifax is one of the largest credit reporting agencies in the United States. In 2017, it suffered a major cybersecurity incident that exposed the personal information of approximately 147 million people.

How did it happen?

Attackers exploited a known vulnerability in Apache Struts, a software framework used by Equifax. A security update was available, but the company had not installed it. This allowed attackers to enter its systems and access sensitive information.

Impact

The stolen information included names, birth dates, addresses and Social Security numbers. Credit card information belonging to approximately 209,000 people was also exposed. The incident caused serious financial and reputational damage to the company.

Why is it important?

The Equifax breach demonstrated the consequences of failing to install important security updates. It also showed that companies responsible for sensitive personal data must protect that information carefully.

3. Marriott Data Breach (2018)

Background

Marriott International is a major hotel company. In 2018, it announced that attackers had accessed the reservation database of Starwood Hotels, which Marriott had acquired in 2016.

How did it happen?

The attackers had access to the Starwood reservation system for several years, beginning in 2014. The intrusion was discovered during a security investigation in 2018. This meant that the attackers had remained inside the system for a long period without being detected.

Impact

Approximately 383 million guest records were initially reported as affected after further investigation. Exposed information included names, contact details, travel information and, in some cases, passport numbers. The incident affected guests from different countries.

Why is it important?

The Marriott incident showed how a security problem in an acquired company can continue to affect a new organization. It also highlighted the importance of checking the security of systems during company acquisitions.

Comparison of the Incidents

Company Year People or Accounts Affected Main Cause
Yahoo 2013 Approximately 3 billion accounts Unauthorized access to user databases
Yahoo 2014 Approximately 500 million accounts Stolen information and forged cookies
Equifax 2017 Approximately 147 million people Unpatched software vulnerability
Marriott 2018 Approximately 383 million guest records Long-term unauthorized system access

These incidents demonstrate that cybersecurity failures can have serious consequences for individuals and companies. Protecting personal information, updating software and monitoring systems are essential to reducing the risk of future data breaches.
